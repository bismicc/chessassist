# Puzzle data

`puzzles.json` is a curated subset of the Lichess puzzle database.

- Source: https://database.lichess.org/#puzzles
- Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
- Curation: puzzles rated 600-2399, popularity at least 78, at least 120 plays, rating deviation at most 100, grouped by motif and rating band.
- Rebuild with `python scripts/build-puzzles.py` after installing `zstandard`.

The puzzle FEN is the position before the opponent's setup move. The first entry in `moves` is that setup move; the rest is the solution. The app applies the setup move before presenting the puzzle.
